## Active Goal Protocol

- `docs/ACTIVE_PHASE.md` is the sole source of truth for the current goal.
- Only one Active Goal is allowed at a time.
- Milestones may contain multiple sequential Goals.
- After a Goal passes, update `ACTIVE_PHASE.md`, commit, and continue to the next queued Goal without asking for confirmation.
- Do not stop for ordinary test failures, implementation errors, or dependency fixes.
- Stop only for irreversible operations, missing external credentials or permissions, or completion of the milestone.
- `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md` records current facts only, not plans or chronological logs.
